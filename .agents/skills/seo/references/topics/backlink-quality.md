# Backlink quality

Validate sampled referring URLs where accessible. Record topical relevance, editorial placement, surrounding text, anchor and destination, and link attributes. Look for repeated schemes across supplied data without treating domain-level metrics as conclusive. An inaccessible referring page can have moved, blocked the host, or failed temporarily.

Separate lost or new links from changing provider coverage and deltas across dates. For suspected spam, cite the observed pattern and evidence limits. Review [Google's link spam guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) before escalating. These signals alone do not justify a disavow.
