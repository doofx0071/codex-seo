# Image briefs and SEO checks

Describe the placement, subject, orientation, composition and crop-safe region. Specify what a person should recognize, the source of factual details, whether branding or product appearance must match a reference, and any accessibility or reuse requirements.

After selecting an asset, verify visual accuracy, fit at relevant sizes and realistic alternative text for the surrounding page. Recommend responsive delivery and an efficient image format appropriate for the content. Check applicable licensing or provenance metadata when it matters.
