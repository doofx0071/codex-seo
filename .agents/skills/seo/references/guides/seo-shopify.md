# Shopify storefront

Sample publicly accessible home, collection, product, variant and policy pages, using the [ecommerce guide](seo-ecommerce.md). Compare preferred URL paths when a product appears under multiple collections. Review canonical and robots tags, public sitemap entries, product JSON-LD and rendered merchandising where accessible.

Theme files or supplied exports can support an implementation review. Clearly label checkout, customer, app configuration, admin and storefront password areas as unavailable without authorized supplied evidence. A sample of public pages is not a crawl inventory. Keep the storefront unchanged.
