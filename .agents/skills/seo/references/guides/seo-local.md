# Local SEO

Identify a physical shop, service-area business, or hybrid from public evidence. Compare name, address and phone where appropriate, service details, GEO references, and hours on the site, visible public listings and supplied exports. Check whether each location has an accurate destination, relevant content, and appropriate LocalBusiness data. Use [local signals](../topics/local-seo-signals.md).

If the host shows public reviews or profiles, cite dated observations without assuming complete history, private fields, or ranking factors. Keep sensitive service-area addresses private where applicable. Recommend fixes for discrepancies and missing evidence. Do not edit any listing.
