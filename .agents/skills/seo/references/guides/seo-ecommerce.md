# Ecommerce SEO

Sample product, collection and category templates. Compare titles and merchandising content, product variants, canonical strategy, faceted navigation, product images, internal links, indexability indicators, and breadcrumbs. Match Product or ProductGroup, Offer, and variant IDs to published page facts. Check price, currency, availability, seller, and review data against the visible page.

Review any user-provided merchant or marketplace export independently. Do not estimate rankings, sellers, visibility, or feed health from a product page. A publicly supplied commerce profile may be examined passively when relevant. Apply [product evidence checks](../topics/product-evidence.md).
