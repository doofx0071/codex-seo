# SEO content brief

Confirm the topic, audience, business fit, intent and intended page type. Sample relevant search results when available, recording query, locale and date. Distinguish direct commercial competitors from editorial or community examples. If search results are unavailable, label intent and gaps as hypotheses.

Use the [brief templates](../topics/page-type-templates.md) to draft the promise, intent, working title and meta description, a useful heading outline, differentiating information, evidence and expert input, related internal links, and success criteria. Plan for original information such as documented steps, comparisons, data, experience, or screenshots. Use relevant terminology naturally rather than quotas.

Respect the site's language and use known site structure to recommend links. Distinguish confirmed existing pages from proposed pages. If the user asks for an outline only, return the headline and structured section list with short notes.
