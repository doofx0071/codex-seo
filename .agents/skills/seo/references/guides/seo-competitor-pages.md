# Competitor and comparison pages

Choose between head-to-head comparison, alternatives, category roundup, and feature matrix by the query and user need. Identify audience, buying criteria, current alternatives and evidence sources. Create a dated comparison table only for verifiable feature, pricing and support details. Label unknown or account-specific items.

Draft tradeoffs, appropriate use cases, buying questions, relevant links and CTAs. Disclose relationships and review evidence when applicable. Avoid unsubstantiated claims about competing products. Suggest ItemList or applicable product types only when the page and [schema guidance](seo-schema.md) support them.
