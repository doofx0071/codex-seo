# Community integrations

Codex SEO incorporates community contributions to SEO clustering, international SEO, drift monitoring, search experience, and provider tooling. [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) lists the original contributors and repositories.

See [MCP and provider setup](MCP-INTEGRATION.md) for supported checkout integrations. To propose a new integration, open a [project issue](https://github.com/helx0071/codex-seo/issues) with provider documentation, credential requirements, the data sent to the provider, and any charges or write operations.
