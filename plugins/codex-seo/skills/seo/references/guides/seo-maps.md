# Maps and business profile review

Confirm the business, relevant search areas, and publicly visible profiles. Check public name, address or service area, phone, site URL, category, hours and reviews where available. Compare representative competitor listings with the same query, location, and search conditions when those conditions are known.

Use [geo-grid evidence](../topics/maps-geo-grid.md) to review a supplied rank grid. Public observations cannot establish aggregate visibility, coverage, or rankings across a city. Label those unavailable without equivalent location-aware evidence. Distinguish public profile facts from fields requiring ownership or access.
