# International SEO and hreflang

Identify representative locale pages and inspect alternates in HTML, HTTP headers, or sitemap XML as available. Check language and optional country codes, fully qualified URLs, self-reference, reciprocal links, canonical alignment, and x-default when appropriate. Treat access and rendered evidence gaps explicitly.

Use [locale content parity](../topics/content-parity.md) to compare equivalent content, legal information, currency and other formats, and page purpose. A user-facing translation difference may be intentional. Draft annotations with verified URLs only and describe how to validate each locale pair.
