# Strategic SEO plan

Gather business priorities, intended markets, site type, template inventory, constraints and the success metric the user can actually track. Use representative evidence from the site and observable competitors to map demand, site structure, content gaps, internal links, and technical dependencies.

Sequence work into stabilization, foundational improvements, content and template expansion, and iteration. For each deliverable specify owner or role, dependency, effort, proof of completion, and KPI where data exists. Label proposed targets as goals. Do not invent baselines or forecast traffic without evidence.
