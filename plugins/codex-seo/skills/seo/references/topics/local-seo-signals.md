# Local SEO evidence

Record business name, public address or service area, phone, hours, website and location identifiers wherever comparable. Keep existing official fields distinct from proposed edits. Check for real local detail on page, relevant categories, review observations and structured data that agrees with the visible facts.

Public results vary by location and time. Use the observed query and locale when comparing competitors. Avoid assuming review averages, rankings, ownership, or completeness from a single screenshot. For applicable LocalBusiness markup, see [Google's local business structured data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business).
