# Experience, expertise, authority and trust

- Experience. Look for original tests, examples, demonstrations, site visits, or other direct involvement relevant to the claim.
- Expertise. Check technical accuracy, relevant qualifications where expected and sufficiently detailed explanations.
- Authority. Check whether verifiable independent sources support the entity or topic. Site-wide reputation may need more external evidence.
- Trust. Confirm who is responsible, how facts were produced, availability of relevant policies, credible sourcing, and consistency of consequential details.

Ask who made the content, how it was produced, and why. Document positive and missing signals with page references. Evaluate claims at the level their stakes require. Apply the criteria as a review rubric without implying a measured ranking factor. See [Google's helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
