# Comparing SEO snapshots

Compare the same URLs, canonical forms, device, capture method and fields. Preserve dates and source. Distinguish unchanged value, changed value, a field observed to be absent, a newly unreachable page, and a field hidden by failed or incomplete capture.

Prioritize verified critical regressions such as blocked indexing, redirects to the wrong destination, or loss of visible primary content on important pages. Compare lab and field metrics separately. A baseline gap should remain unavailable until captured; do not treat it as zero or as a regression.
